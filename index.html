About Me

My interest in semiconductor devices developed during my undergraduate studies in Avionics Engineering, where I built a foundation in electronic circuits, VLSI design, control systems, signal processing, and embedded technologies.

Since 2023, I have been involved in simulation-based research on semiconductor and photovoltaic devices. My work includes nanoscale transistor modeling, defect and interface analysis, perovskite solar-cell optimization, automated dataset generation, and machine learning-assisted performance evaluation.

I plan to pursue advanced graduate research in semiconductor device physics, low-power transistor architectures, TCAD-based modeling, and emerging electronic technologies for future integrated circuits and aerospace applications.

Practical Demonstrations

Featured Technical Projects

Design, Simulation and Physical Verification of a CMOS Inverter Using Cadence Virtuoso

Cadence Virtuoso CMOS Inverter Transient Analysis Physical Layout DRC / LVS

A CMOS inverter was designed using PMOS and NMOS transistors in Cadence Virtuoso. The project included transistor-level schematic design, transient simulation, and physical layout implementation. The simulated output showed the expected inverted response, where a high input produced a low output and a low input produced a high output. The schematic, waveform, and layout were successfully completed and verified.

TCAD-Based Design and Electrical Characterization of a Nanoscale FinFET Device

Silvaco ATLAS 3D FinFET Id–Vg Analysis Subthreshold Slope DIBL

A 20 nm FinFET was designed and simulated using Silvaco ATLAS. The device showed proper switching with Vth ≈ 0.30 V, Ion of 2.918 × 10−5 A at 0.1 V and 3.214 × 10−4 A at 1 V, while Ioff remained near 10−10 A. The subthreshold slope was 76–76.8 mV/dec and DIBL was about 0.056 V/V.

Design and Performance Analysis of a Cs₃Bi₂I₉/Silicon Tandem Solar Cell Using SCAPS-1D

SCAPS-1D Tandem Solar Cell Cs₃Bi₂I₉ Silicon Bottom Cell J–V / QE Analysis

A Cs₃Bi₂I₉/silicon tandem solar cell was designed and simulated using SCAPS-1D. The device achieved a Jsc of 43.34 mA/cm², Voc of 0.80 V, fill factor of 83.9%, and efficiency of 29.44% without bulk defects. When bulk defects were considered, the efficiency decreased to 21.13%, mainly due to reductions in Voc and fill factor.
